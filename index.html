Lạc.

Lạc (Vietnamese: lost) gives you a deck of walking rules and sends you out without a map. At each stop you take one photo and record ten seconds of sound. When you get home, an open model on your own computer looks and listens, and turns the walk into a small zine and a one-minute soundwalk.

score loaded

Before you go: switch on airplane mode. Lạc works offline; nothing you record leaves this phone until you choose to send it home.
